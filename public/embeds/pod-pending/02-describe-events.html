Step 1: read the events

describe, not logs

A Pending Pod has no container, so logs are empty. The scheduler writes its reasoning into the Pod's Events. Scroll to the bottom and read it, reason by reason.

kubectl describe pod · Events 
$ kubectl describe pod api-7d9f # scroll to the bottom: the scheduler talks hereEvents:  Warning  FailedScheduling  0/12 nodes are available:3 Insufficient cpu, resources4 node(s) had untolerated taint(s), taints5 node(s) didn't match Pod's node affinity/selector. affinity

One FailedScheduling line, broken down by reason. Each reason points to one of the four checks. The scheduler already did the diagnosis. You just read it.
