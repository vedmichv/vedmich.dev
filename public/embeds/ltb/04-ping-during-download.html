Ping while you download.

This is the single most useful test you can run. Keep a ping going, then start a big download. If the line stays flat, your slowness is elsewhere. If it balloons the moment traffic starts, that is bufferbloat: the cure is a shorter queue, not a bigger plan. Start a download and watch.
