The latency tax on a 1 Gbps link.

A single TCP connection can have only one window of data in flight per round trip (the smaller of cwnd and rwnd). So its throughput is capped at window size divided by RTT, no matter how fat the pipe. Drag the round-trip time up and watch a gigabit link collapse.
