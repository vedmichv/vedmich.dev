A hands-on workshop on AWS DevOps Agent, based on the public AWS workshop [AWS DevOps Agent Workshop](https://catalog.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/workshops/767d3081-b4fa-4e08-81da-17e5c94a1a08/en-US), which you can also run on your own. It teaches how to use the agent to troubleshoot and resolve operational issues in serverless and containerized applications and in AI/ML services. Its modules are self-contained and can be done in any order. They cover Lambda and DynamoDB throttling, ECS and EKS microservices, and investigations with third-party telemetry such as Splunk and Grafana. Other modules cover failures in SageMaker, Bedrock and other AI/ML services, and in Step Functions, Glue, EMR Serverless and Redshift pipelines. The learning objectives also include setting up the agent with Agent Spaces and access controls.

A two-hour session on the workshop day of [AWS Community Day Malta 2026](https://www.awscommunityday.mt/), Thursday 15 October 2026, 15:30-17:30, in Ballroom B at the Hard Rock Hotel Malta in St Julian's. The talks day followed on 16 October.
