Most companies already use AI somewhere, and most are still stuck in pilots. [Sergey Kurson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sergey-kurson/) opened with why AI maturity, not technology, separates the organizations that scale AI from the ones that do not.

My part: the model is not the bottleneck. Mature companies run an adoption practice instead of a pile of disconnected pilots. Of the six AI maturity dimensions only one is technical, and your maturity is the minimum across them, not the average, so Level 3 on all six beats Level 5 on one. Then four shifts that agentic AI forces (culture, governance, risk and organization, after Ishit Vachhrajani's "From Automation to Agency"), the adoption loop with a business number agreed before the first line of code and a real go/no-go decision (a pilot without the right to say no is not an experiment, it is a subscription), and a prioritization matrix for what to do on Monday.

Co-presented with Sergey Kurson, Principal Solutions Architect at AWS, on Stage 1 (Vision Track) of the first Tech Race Summit by SOFTSWISS at Centrum Praskie Koneser, Warsaw.
