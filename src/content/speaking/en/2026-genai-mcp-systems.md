A demo-driven journey from GenAI basics to production multi-agent systems: single-agent architectures first, then the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for standardized context handling, shown through Kubernetes troubleshooting and Terraform generation demos, and finally multi-agent orchestration with Amazon Bedrock and Strands Agents.

Data & AI track at Code Europe 2025 (EXPO Kraków, 30 June to 1 July 2025). The recording is on the [Code Europe YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxZiNWUCl3Q).
