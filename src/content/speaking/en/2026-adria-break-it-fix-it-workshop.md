A hands-on workshop on autonomous incident response. Participants deploy a small production-style application on AWS and set up AWS DevOps Agent. Then they break the application on purpose with a bad deployment, a misconfiguration and a failing dependency. The agent investigates on its own. It correlates telemetry across CloudWatch and logs, walks the topology of the application, finds the root cause and proposes a mitigation. By the end, participants will have run a full loop from alert to root cause to mitigation with an autonomous agent. They will also understand how to connect the agent to their own observability and code, and leave with a working reference setup to adapt. The application, the broken scenarios and the guardrails are provided. No prior experience with AWS DevOps Agent is needed. Attendees bring their own laptop.

A two-hour workshop at [AWS Community Day Adria 2026](https://www.awscommunityadria.com/sessions/ljubljana-1267865/) on 22 October 2026, 13:15-15:15, in room Ravnikar at the Four Points by Sheraton Ljubljana Mons in Ljubljana, Slovenia.
