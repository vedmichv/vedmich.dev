A 30-minute session at [AWS Community Day Adria 2026](https://www.awscommunityadria.com/sessions/ljubljana-rca-agent/) on 22 October 2026, in room Plečnik 5 at the Four Points by Sheraton Ljubljana Mons in Ljubljana, Slovenia. It repeats the talk from [Tashkent](/en/speaking/2026-rca-agent-agentcore/) with the same deck, so the slides link is the Tashkent version. The recording of the Tashkent session is on that page.
