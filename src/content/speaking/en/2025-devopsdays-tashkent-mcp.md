Generative AI has outgrown chatbots and has become a tool in the DevOps toolkit. The talk shows how to use the Model Context Protocol (MCP) in day-to-day work: agents that troubleshoot Kubernetes through MCP, pull live AWS documentation, analyze pricing and take over repetitive tasks. It also gives a clear mental model of the MCP ecosystem: what it is, how to use it, and how to build your own MCP server. For engineers who are done with the hype and want to see how MCP and GenAI change daily operations today.

[devopsdays Tashkent 2025](https://devopsdays.org/events/2025-tashkent/welcome/), a one-day community conference on Saturday 24 May 2025 at Digital.uz in Tashkent. The talk was delivered in Russian.
